# LUCCI Trader's Edge Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

## Overview

LUCCI Trader's Edge is a private personal knowledge and decision-support system.

This privacy policy explains how LUCCI handles Google Drive data accessed through OAuth.

## Information Accessed

When authorized, LUCCI may access Google Drive files using:

`https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file`

This permission allows LUCCI to create and manage files created by the application.

LUCCI does not request unrestricted access to a user's entire Google Drive.

## How Data Is Used

Authorized Drive access is used only to:

- save LUCCI-generated derived files;
- maintain application state;
- preserve user-owned outputs.

LUCCI does not sell, publish, or share private user information with advertisers.

## Storage

Private trading materials, recordings, transcripts, and generated knowledge artifacts remain private.

Credentials and OAuth tokens are stored using secure secret-management practices.

## Data Deletion

Users may revoke LUCCI access at any time through Google Account permissions.
